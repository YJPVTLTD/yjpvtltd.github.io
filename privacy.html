Privacy Policy

Updated 14 November 2026. This version takes effect on 1 December 2026, the day SWARNIX early access is released. Until then the previous version applies to bookings already made.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the waitlist, sell and deliver SWARNIX, and support you: mainly your email address and what you bought.
  • SWARNIX has no telemetry, analytics or advertising. It contacts our servers to check for and download updates, and to activate your license and check it once a day. It never sends your files, your apps or how you use your PC.
  • The website has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context advertising.

This policy explains what personal data Swarnix Technologies ("Velotic", "we", "us") collects through the SWARNIX website at swarnix.in, the SWARNIX waitlist, SWARNIX purchases and early access bookings, the SWARNIX jewelry billing and ERP software, our emails and our support (together, the "Services"), why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. Swarnix Technologies, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States, is the controller of this data.

1. What we collect

1.1 When you join the waitlist

Your email address, the country your connection comes from, the address of the page you signed up on (which can include a tag such as ?ref=youtube showing where you came from), your browser's user agent and the time you signed up.

1.2 When you buy or book SWARNIX

Stripe, our payment processor, runs the checkout. It collects your name, email address, billing address and payment details, and processes the payment under its own privacy policy. From Stripe we receive your name, email address, billing address, order number, what you bought, the amount, currency and date, and where you came from if the link had a tag. Before you pay, we check your email address against our list of buyers blocked for past payment disputes or fraud. We never receive your full card or bank details.

1.3 When you download SWARNIX

We record when the download link for your purchase is used, with the IP address and the time. We use these records to deliver your download, prevent link sharing, and decide refund and withdrawal requests (see the Refund Policy).

1.4 When you use SWARNIX

SWARNIX has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising. It contacts our servers for two things:

  • Updates. It checks our update server (updates.swarnix.in) for updates and downloads them.
  • License activation and a daily license check. When you activate SWARNIX, and then once a day, it checks your license with our license server. It sends your product key (only when you activate SWARNIX or move it to another PC); one-way SHA-256 hashes of three hardware identifiers (the motherboard's ID, the processor model and the serial number of the disk SWARNIX starts from), never the identifiers themselves; the public half of a key SWARNIX creates on your PC; your activation ticket; and the SWARNIX version. Downloads from our servers also carry a download pass that proves your license.

SWARNIX sends nothing else: no name, email address, account, location, files, list of installed apps or usage. Our servers see your IP address as part of each connection. The license server doesn't store IP addresses; for rate limits it keeps a keyed hash of the address, deleted within a day. Your files, settings and app data stay on your PC.

When you install apps through SWARNIX, it downloads them from the sources you choose, such as Flathub or an app's publisher. Those sources see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies.

1.5 When you contact us

Your name, email address and the messages you send to support.

1.6 Technical data

Our hosting provider records requests to the website (IP address, browser, address requested and time) to run and protect it.

1.7 Unsubscribing

If you unsubscribe from our emails, we keep your email address on a list of addresses that must not receive them.

2. Why we use it, and our legal bases

  • To run the waitlist and tell you when early access opens: your consent, which you give by signing up and can withdraw at any time.
  • To take payment, deliver SWARNIX and your license key, activate and check your license, and provide updates: performance of our contract with you.
  • To send receipts, delivery emails, release-date changes and important notices: contract, and our legitimate interest in keeping customers informed.
  • To count where sign-ups and orders come from (for example, a YouTube video): our legitimate interest in understanding which channels work.
  • To prevent fraud, piracy, link sharing and chargeback abuse, and to decide refund requests using download records: our legitimate interest in protecting our business and customers.
  • To answer support requests: contract or legitimate interest.
  • To keep financial and tax records: legal obligation.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers who process data for us under contract, only to run the Services:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, the license server, the waitlist backup copy, downloads, security and request logs;
  • Supabase: our database of waitlist sign-ups and customers;
  • Stripe: checkout, payments, receipts, refunds and fraud prevention. For some data, such as fraud checks, it also acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy;
  • an email delivery provider, for the emails we send you.

We also disclose data when the law requires it, to protect our rights or others' safety, or to a successor if our business is sold or merged. We do not sell personal data.

4. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers store and process data in the United States and other countries. When we transfer personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on our providers' data processing agreements with the Standard Contractual Clauses, or on their certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, where applicable.

5. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist sign-ups: until early access opens and we have told you, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.
  • Customer and order data: while you are a customer. If you ask us to delete it, we do so within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
  • License records (a hash of your product key, its last four characters, the hardware hashes of the PC it is activated on, and activation and check dates): for the life of the license.
  • Purchase and invoice records: as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 7 years).
  • Download records: up to 2 years, or longer while a refund request or payment dispute about that purchase is open.
  • Support messages: up to 2 years after you send them.
  • Unsubscribe list: for as long as we send emails, so your choice keeps working.
  • Hosting request logs: a few days.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The SWARNIX website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising scripts. When you open the checkout, Stripe loads its checkout script and sets its own cookies to process payment and prevent fraud. Because we don't track visitors across websites or sell or share personal data, we treat Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals the same way as every other visit: there is nothing to opt out of.

7. Security

Data travels only over encrypted connections. Our database is accessed only by our servers and authorised staff, and the website holds no database keys. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data, or a portable copy in a machine-readable format;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to our processing, including for direct marketing (always honoured);
  • withdraw consent you gave, without affecting earlier processing.

We give these rights to everyone, including residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, and we do not treat you differently for using them. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising and do not use sensitive personal data to infer characteristics about you.

To use your rights, email privacy@swarnix.in from the email address you used with us. We answer within 30 days (45 days where U.S. state law allows). If we decline, you can appeal by replying to our answer. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal data, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, contact privacy@swarnix.in.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new effective date. For material changes we will also notify customers and waitlist members by email before they take effect.
