The short version

This policy is part of the SWARNIX Terms of Service. It covers every SWARNIX purchase from Swarnix Technologies ("we", "us"), including early access bookings. Our payment processor, Stripe, processes payments and refunds.

1. Early access bookings, before release

When you book early access, you pay now and get SWARNIX when early access opens. Until then, you can cancel your booking and get a full refund at any time, for any reason. You don't need to explain why.

If we move the release date, we will email you. You can then keep your booking or cancel it for a full refund. If we cancel SWARNIX or can't deliver it, we will refund every booking in full without you having to ask.

2. After release

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days, as long as you have not downloaded the SWARNIX installer (the ISO file). The 14 days start:

  • on the day you buy, for purchases made after release; or

3. When a purchase counts as used

A purchase is used, and can no longer be refunded, once the SWARNIX installer has been downloaded with it. Downloading SWARNIX gives you the full product, which can't be returned. Our download records, which show when the download link for your purchase was used, decide this (see the Privacy Policy).

If you live where the law gives consumers a right to withdraw from online purchases, you can withdraw from your purchase until you download SWARNIX. Nothing in this policy limits the rights consumer law gives you if SWARNIX is faulty or not as described. Those rights still apply after you download it.

Email support@swarnix.in from the email address you bought with, and include your order number from your receipt. We answer within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method; banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show them.
